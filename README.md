## 📋 About

This is the source repository for my personal academic website. The site showcases my research philosophy, publications, and thoughts on conducting meaningful AI research with integrity and transparency.

### Core Philosophy

- **Transparency and open access** — No paywalls, no gatekeeping
- **Iterative, reproducible science** — Process over prestige
- **Welcoming feedback** — Post-publication dialogue matters
- **Valuing null results** — All contributions advance science
- **Accessibility** — Research for anyone curious about the field
